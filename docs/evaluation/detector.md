# Detector evaluation and edge benchmarks

Generated by `make edge-eval`: COCO val2017 (5000 images); INT8 calibrated on the first 128, 640x640, CPU only (arm64 arm). Latency is the network alone, single image, after warm-up, measured on an otherwise idle machine.

| ONNX FP32 | 9.94 | 19.83 | 21.86 | 50.4 | 0.397 | 0.513 | 0.439 | 0.604 | 0.674 |
| ONNX INT8 | 3.09 | 21.98 | 23.58 | 45.5 | 0.364 | 0.484 | 0.402 | 0.573 | 0.672 |

PIDS class AP is the mean COCO AP50-95 of its member classes (vehicle: car, motorcycle, bus, truck; animal: ten COCO animal classes). Recall is Ultralytics' per-class recall at the F1-optimal confidence.
