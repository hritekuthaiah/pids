# SOP-03 False alarms

## Animals
Dogs, cattle and birds are the most frequent false-alarm source. If the camera confirms an
animal and the thermal channel agrees, resolve the alert as a false alarm with the note
"animal". Repeated animal entries at one point indicate a fence gap: raise a maintenance
ticket.

## Ghost tracks and glare
Short-lived tracks that vanish within seconds, usually from foliage, glare or rain, are
resolved as false alarms after a camera review. If a ghost track recurs at the same spot,
report the camera for cleaning or re-aiming.

## Public road traffic
Pedestrians and vehicles on the public road inside the warning band are not intrusions while
they keep moving along the road. Escalate only if they stop near the fence or leave the road
toward the compound.
