# SOP-06 Escalation and incident reporting

## Escalation
CRITICAL alerts are reported to the officer in charge immediately by radio, then by phone.
Two or more simultaneous WARNING alerts at different points are escalated as a possible
coordinated probe.

## Incident report contents
Every confirmed intrusion or resolved CRITICAL alert needs an incident report within the shift:
time of first alert, zone, description of the person or vehicle, guard actions, outcome,
and any equipment issues. Attach the alert id so the system's evidence can be reviewed.

## Closing an alert
Resolve an alert only after a guard or the operator has confirmed the outcome. Use "false
alarm" with a reason when nothing was there, so the system's nuisance statistics stay honest.
