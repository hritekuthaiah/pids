# SOP-02 Alarm assessment

## Assessing a WARNING alert
A WARNING alert means behaviour or position worth a human look, not yet a confirmed
intrusion. The operator reviews the live camera, the track's recent path and the fusion
explanation, then decides within 60 seconds: dispatch a guard to observe, keep watching, or
mark it a false alarm with a reason.

## Loitering near the fence
A person dwelling near the perimeter fence, or walking parallel to it, is a common sign of
reconnaissance. Dispatch a guard to observe from inside the fence and log a description.
Do not ignore repeated visits by the same person across a shift.

## Night-time activity
Movement in the warning band between 22:00 and 05:00 lowers the threshold for dispatch:
send a guard to observe any person who is not a tagged patrol.
