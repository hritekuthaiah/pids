# SOP-04 Identity and tag anomalies

## Suspected GPS spoofing
If a GPS tag reports positions that jump impossibly, or claims to be in view of a camera that
sees no one there, treat the tag as compromised: do not let it whitelist anyone. Verify the
patrol's real position by radio, and if the patrol cannot be raised, treat the area as
having an intruder.

## Unknown or revoked badge
A badge or tag that is not in the registry, or has been revoked, gives no authorisation.
Respond as for an unidentified person and record the tag id for the incident report.

## Badge heard but nobody seen
A phone badge heard by the scanner with no person on camera may be a badge carried just out
of view or a replayed signal. No action is needed unless it coincides with an alert;
then verify the badge holder's location by radio.
