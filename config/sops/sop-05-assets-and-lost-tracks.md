# SOP-05 Assets and lost tracks

## Asset leaving its containment zone
A tagged asset (for example a stores crate) leaving its containment zone is a theft in
progress until shown otherwise. Close the vehicle gate, dispatch the nearest guard to the
asset's last position, and notify the officer in charge.

## Lost track inside the compound
When the cameras lose a contact inside the compound, send a guard to search from the last
known position along the predicted path. Check blind spots next to buildings first. Keep the
alert open until the contact is found or the area is cleared.
