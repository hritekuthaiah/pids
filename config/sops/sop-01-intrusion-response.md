# SOP-01 Intrusion response

## Critical zone breach by an unidentified person
A CRITICAL alert means an untagged person is inside a critical zone. Dispatch the nearest
guard immediately and keep a second guard on the main gate. The control-room operator keeps
the person on camera and talks the responder in by radio. The responding guard must not
engage alone; they observe, challenge from a safe distance, and wait for backup.

## Critical zone breach by a vehicle
Treat an untagged vehicle inside the compound as hostile until identified. Close the vehicle
gate, alert the quick-reaction team, and keep guards out of its path. Record the
registration and direction of travel from the camera feed.

## Predicted breach (warning before entry)
When the system predicts a breach within seconds, move a guard to the predicted entry point
before the person arrives. Prevention beats pursuit: a challenge at the fence line resolves
most incidents before entry.

## Authorised person inside a critical zone
A tagged, authorised person in a critical zone is expected during duty hours. If the badge
or GPS tag is flagged as suspicious, or it is outside duty hours, verify identity by radio
before standing down.
